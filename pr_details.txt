Title: ⚡ Optimize weekly and monthly contributions calculation loop

Body:
💡 **What:** Replaced the two separate `filter().reduce()` loops with a single reverse `for` loop that computes both `weeklyContributions` and `monthlyContributions` in a single pass. Also avoids unnecessary `Date` instantiations by stopping the loop early since the array is already sorted.
🎯 **Why:** To eliminate redundant iterations over the calendar array, reducing GC pressure and CPU usage by avoiding intermediate array allocations from `.filter()`.
📊 **Measured Improvement:** In a local benchmark script comparing the original method versus the optimized reverse-loop method with 100,000 iterations:
- Original: ~34,386 ms
- Optimized: ~2,016 ms
- Improvement: ~94% faster (17x speedup)
